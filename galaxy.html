Galaxies

Sr. No. Name Description
1 Milky Way The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy that contains our solar system, with over 100 billion stars. It spans about 100,000 light-years across and is visible from Earth as a bright band in the night sky.
2 Antennae Galaxy The Antennae Galaxies are a pair of interacting spiral galaxies, located about 45 million light-years away. Their dramatic appearance, with long tidal tails, is a result of a collision and merger process.
3 Cigar Galaxy The Cigar Galaxy (NGC 4565) is an edge-on spiral galaxy located about 40 million light-years away. Its distinct elongated shape, resembling a cigar, makes it one of the most striking galaxies in the sky.
4 Butterfly Galaxy The Butterfly Galaxy (NGC 4567 and NGC 4568) is a pair of interacting galaxies located about 60 million light-years away. Their interaction has caused them to take on a butterfly-like shape, with long, sweeping tidal tails.
5 Whirlpool Galaxy The Whirlpool Galaxy (M51) is a spiral galaxy located about 23 million light-years away. It is famous for its distinct, well-defined spiral arms and its interaction with a smaller galaxy, NGC 5195, which creates a stunning, cosmic "whirlpool" appearance.
